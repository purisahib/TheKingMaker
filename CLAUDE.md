## Project Overview

This is a creative writing project — a long-form epic novel titled **"The Rise of Samrat: The King Maker (सम्राट का उदय: राजा बनाने वाला)"**, written in pure Hindi. There is no software codebase; the repository contains the story prompt and generated story pages.

## Key File

- `Prompts` — Master brief containing all story requirements, structure rules, and creative direction.

## Writing Rules

- **Language**: Pure Hindi (हिंदी में लिखना है — Hinglish नहीं, पूरी कहानी हिंदी में होगी).
- **Length target**: 800 pages, each page 3000+ words. Write page by page — never summarize or skip ahead.
- **Setting**: Ancient/medieval Indian-style kingdom using proper Hindi terms (राज्य, राजा, महाराज, सेनापति, राजकुमारी, etc.).
- **Genre**: Action, Drama, Romance, War, Politics, Betrayal.
- **Tone**: Cinematic, dark, emotional, powerful — a mix of hope, revenge, love, and destiny.

## Page Structure

Every page must include:
1. Main story progression
2. Strong, natural dialogues
3. Detailed scene description
4. Emotional depth

End every page with:
```
👉 Next Page Preview:
```
followed by a brief suspenseful hint of what comes next.

## Story Arc & Pacing

- **Early pages**: World building + character introductions (dark, mysterious opening with a prophecy)
- **Mid pages**: Conflict, war, love, betrayal
- **Final pages**: Climax and ultimate twist

Build slowly — no rushing. Maintain strong continuity across all pages.

## Hero Backstory

- Marthe's parents were killed when he was very young — this is the central wound of the story.
- During that attack, his **rakth raksha** (a mystical blood-based protection/power) was shattered.
- Because of the broken rakth raksha, his body is permanently weakened — he can never gain physical strength.
- He was raised alone by his Dada ji (grandfather).
- His journey is one of overcoming physical limitation through mind, strategy, and willpower.

## Continuity Rules

- Never skip pages or summarize future events.
- Track character arcs, plot threads, and established world details across pages.
- Before writing a new page, review the previous page(s) to maintain consistency.
